# Bloodlinez playtest: what to do

Thanks for playing. This is an early test of a browser detective game. You're testing the game, not your own skill. If something confuses you, that's the most useful thing you can tell us.

## What it is

You work the night shift at a probate firm. A rich man has drowned and three people want his estate. You read old records, build the family tree yourself, and file findings with the firm's partners. Everything happens on three websites inside the game: a genealogy site, your work email and the firm's intranet.

## Before you start

1. Use a laptop or desktop with Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. A phone works but is harder.
2. Set aside about 90 minutes. You can stop and come back; the game saves in your browser.
3. Open the game at the link you were sent and enter the password you were given. Your browser remembers it after that.
4. Turn on the log. In the game, open the **Matter 2025-0417** tab, go to **Overview**, and press **Start recording** in the Playtest log box. (If your link ends in `?playtest=1` it is already on.)

The log records what you search, open, link and file, with times. It stays on your computer until you send it. It records nothing outside the game.

## While you play

- Play the way you would at home. Don't look things up outside the game.
- If you can, think aloud or jot down a line when you're stuck, surprised or annoyed. Note the time.
- The **Field guide** (top right) explains the tools. It does not contain answers.
- Investigation mode allows revisions. The optional challenge has three final filings. Preliminary reports do not spend a final filing.

## When you finish (or give up)

1. Go back to **Matter 2025-0417 › Overview** and press **Save log file**. A file named `bloodlinez-playtest-….json` downloads.
2. Send that file, plus any notes, to the person who invited you.
3. Answer the short questions they send you. Please do it the same day while it's fresh.

Please don't tell other testers anything about the case until they've played.

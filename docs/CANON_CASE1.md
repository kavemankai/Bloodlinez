# Case 1 canon: the four lives of Ambrose Vane (spoilers)

This is the single source of truth for Case 1. Records, findings, tests and art follow it. If a record disagrees with this file, the record is wrong.

## The rule he lives by

Ambrose Vane was turned on 14 February 1888 and has looked about 31 ever since. A paper identity has to age, so roughly every forty years he hands his life on to an heir. The heir is never his own child; after the turning he cannot father one. In each life he marries a widow who has a child by an earlier marriage, or a child is adopted into the house. When the time comes, he stages his own death and takes over the heir's identity at about the heir's real age.

Each handover comes with a disaster that kills the people who would notice the swap: the wife and the real heir. The records never say he caused them. The player sees the same convenient tragedy three times and draws their own conclusion. Anyone who knows the secret and keeps it is spared. Harriet, his only real child, is spared.

Each staged death is different:

| Year | Staged death | What the records got wrong |
|---|---|---|
| 1934 | Fire at Vane House | A burned body identified as Ambrose by his signet ring. It was his stepson Desmond. |
| 1976 | Car over the cliff at Gull Rock | The driver's body, identified by the surviving son, is recorded as Desmond. It was the son, Cornelius. |
| 2025 | Lost from the *Marguerite* at sea | No bodies. Cornelius and his wife Helen are recorded as drowned. The real grandson Julian drowned too; the man who came home as Julian is Ambrose. |

He has lived at Vane House, 14 Hollow Lane, Ashby, the whole time. Ashgrove & Pell handles every handover.

## The blood line (who inherits)

Ambrose → Harriet (b. 1888, conceived before the turning) → Thomas Holloway (b. 1920) → Margaret Holloway (b. 1951). Under Accord Art. 4 a staged death returns the estate to the issue of the blood: Margaret. Desmond, Cornelius and Julian were never his blood. Neither is Daphne.

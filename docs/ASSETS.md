# Image assets

The rule that matters most: the vampire must be recognisably the same man across five photos and 130 years. His scar through the left eyebrow and the mole on his lower cheek sit in the same place in every one. The photo lab depends on this. Make a character reference sheet first and generate every photo of him from it.

## 1. Case photos (must have)

| File | Shows | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `ambrose_1889.jpg` | Studio cabinet portrait, about 32, high collar, sepia | Lamplit, dark backdrop. Scar and mole clear. |
| `wedding_1912.jpg` | Groom Arthur Holloway (moustache, heavier build) beside the bride's father, the vampire, still about 33 | Landscape. He's obviously too young to be her father. |
| `cornelius_1962_news.jpg` | Host at a ball, newspaper halftone | Black and white with a dot screen, dinner suit. |
| `cornelius_1972_licence.jpg` | Licence-booth headshot, 70s hair and collar | Faded colour, flat flash. |
| `julian_2019_licence.jpg` | Modern licence headshot, short hair | Grey backdrop, harsh flash. |
| `thomas_1950.jpg` | Thomas Holloway, about 30, glasses, wavy hair | Black and white. Mole, no scar. This is the red herring. |

## 2. Avatars

Margaret (70s, recent), Daphne (about 59, recent), Arthur (crop from the wedding photo), Eliza (Victorian, about 30, sepia), Harriet (young woman around 1912, sepia). Desmond gets no photo. That's deliberate.

## 3. Document textures (should have)

Documents stay as HTML text. These go behind them:

- `paper_registry.jpg`: register form with a faint ruled grid
- `paper_newsprint.jpg`: yellowed newsprint
- `paper_letter.jpg`: modern cream letter paper
- `paper_diary.jpg`: diary page with a binding shadow and foxing
- `licence_card_1972.png` and `licence_card_2019.png`: blank card templates with the photo slot, no text
- `stamp_registrar.png`: transparent round "District of Ashby" seal

## 4. Site branding (nice to have)

`bloodlines_logo.svg`, `ap_crest.svg`, `bl_hero_home.jpg` (album or archive drawers), `dna_banner.jpg` (DNA strand on dark red), and about six `collection_*.jpg` thumbnails.

## 5. Story payoffs (later)

- `cellar_coffins.jpg`: Margaret's torch-lit phone photo of four coffins, for her end-of-case email
- `ashgrove_plate.jpg`: close-up of the Ashgrove brass plate (copperplate script)

## Specs

- Photos 600×750 (4:5), the wedding photo 1200×750, avatars 256×256.
- JPEG at about 150 KB each.
- Once the photos exist, record the pixel position of the scar and the mole on each vampire photo, and of the mole on Thomas. The photo lab's click test needs these.
